## About the Dataset

### Treadmill Maximal Exercise Tests from the Exercise Physiology and Human Performance Lab of the University of Malaga
- This dataset (Treadmill Exercise Cardiorespiratory Dataset) comprises cardiorespiratory measurements from 992 maximal graded exercise tests conducted on amateur and professional athletes aged 10-63.
- The tests, performed between 2008-2018. include breath-by-breath measurements of heart rate, oxygen consumption, carbon dioxide generation, and pulmonary ventilation, along with treadmill speed. Participant characteristics, environmental conditions, and test protocols are also provided.
- This comprehensive dataset offers valuable insights into exercise physiology and human performance, with potential applications in sports science, medicine, and health research.
- Access to the dataset at PhysioNet TREADMILL "https://physionet.org/content/treadmill-exercise-cardioresp/1.0.1/#files"

### CSV Files
- subject-info.csv : Particpants information i.e professional athletes aged 10-63.
- test_measure.csv : Measurements taken when walking running on treadmill
